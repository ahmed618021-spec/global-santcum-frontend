Welcome

Serenity Day Spa is Brisbane's premier urban wellness destination, offering a tranquil escape from the demands of daily life. Nestled in the heart of the city, our award-winning spa combines ancient healing traditions with modern therapeutic techniques.

Every visit is designed to restore balance to body, mind, and spirit. From our signature thermal circuit to our expert massage therapists, we invite you to surrender to stillness and emerge renewed.

Policies

Cancellation Policy

  • 48+ hours before appointment: Full refund
  • 24-48 hours before: 50% refund
  • Less than 24 hours: No refund

Rescheduling: Bookings can be rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment, subject to availability.

Arrival

Please arrive 15 minutes before your appointment to complete check-in and prepare for your treatment. This allows time to change, store belongings, and begin relaxing before your service.

  • Late arrivals: Your treatment may be shortened to accommodate subsequent bookings
  • No-shows: Full charge applies

Health & Safety

Please inform us of any health conditions, allergies, injuries, or pregnancy when booking. Some treatments have contraindications.

  • Thermal facilities not recommended for pregnant women
  • Some treatments not suitable for certain medical conditions
  • Please consult your doctor if unsure

Venue Etiquette

  • Quiet zones: Please maintain a peaceful atmosphere throughout the spa
  • Mobile phones: Silent mode required; no calls in common areas
  • Photography: Not permitted in treatment areas or changing rooms
  • Dress code: Swimwear required in thermal facilities; robes provided for all other areas

Payment

  • Accepted: Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, EFTPOS, Cash
  • Gift vouchers: Valid for 3 years from purchase date
