How It Works

Your Journey with The Global Sanctum

Discover how we connect wellness travelers and retreat hosts with exceptional venues worldwide. A seamless experience from discovery to booking.

For Wellness Travelers

Find Your Sanctuary

1

Discover

Browse our curated collection of wellness venues and retreat spaces. Filter by location, experience type, amenities, and more to find your perfect match.

2

Explore

View detailed venue profiles with high-resolution galleries, comprehensive facility information, services offered, and authentic reviews.

3

Connect

Reach out to venues directly through our platform. Ask questions, check availability, and discuss your specific needs.

4

Book

Reserve your experience with confidence. Secure payment, instant confirmation, and flexible cancellation options.

For Retreat Hosts

Host Your Transformation

1

Search

Find the perfect venue for your retreat program. Filter by capacity, facilities, location, and specific wellness modalities.

2

Compare

Evaluate venues side by side. Review detailed information about spaces, accommodation, catering options, and past host reviews.

3

Enquire

Submit your retreat requirements. Our team works with you and the venue to coordinate dates, group size, and special arrangements.

4

Confirm

Secure your venue with a deposit. We handle the logistics so you can focus on creating an exceptional retreat experience.

For Venue Owners

Showcase Your Space

1

Apply

Submit your venue for review. We curate all listings to ensure quality and alignment with our wellness community.

2

Create

Build your comprehensive venue profile. Upload photos, describe your facilities, and highlight what makes your space unique.

3

Connect

Receive enquiries from qualified retreat hosts and wellness guests. Our platform facilitates direct communication.

4

Grow

Manage bookings through your dashboard. Track performance, update availability, and build your reputation in the wellness community.
