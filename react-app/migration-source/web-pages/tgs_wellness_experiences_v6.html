How it works

Browse by modality,
find your venue.

Each practice links directly to venues in our collection offering that modality. No duplication — the experience lives at the venue.

Why categories

Depth over
breadth.

Rather than listing every treatment, we group by tradition and lineage. This helps you understand the roots of each practice and find venues that go deep, not wide.

Our approach

Curated with
intention.

Every venue in our collection is personally vetted. When you click through to a practice, you're seeing only spaces we'd recommend to someone we care about.
